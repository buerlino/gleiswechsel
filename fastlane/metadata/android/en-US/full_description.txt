Gleiswechsel checks whether your daily train trip in Switzerland has a faster connection than the official timetable shows.

The official planners only show a change of trains if it takes at least the station's official track switch time, which is generous at big stations. Gleiswechsel uses your own track switch time instead and looks for the connections that were left out.

Its full search also tries routes through other stations, in a file of two weeks of Swiss trains that it downloads about once a week (about 0.5 MB).

The fastest of the day (the all day button) shows the fastest connection of a weekday with your track switch times, and how often it runs.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your commutes stay on your phone.

Data: Swiss public transport open data (opentransportdata.swiss, transport.opendata.ch; the timetable file from buerlino.github.io). Not affiliated with SBB or any other transport company.
